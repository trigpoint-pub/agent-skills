## What the skill adds on its own

These are working choices from Trigpoint (2026), which wrote the skill. Where a choice adapts one of the references above, the entry says so:

- **The wording of the four categories.** The skill summarises them; the paper's verbatim definitions are not quoted, and the node this skill relies on checked the definitions through secondary records rather than the full text.
- **The abuse checklist in Step 3** and the **fix table in Step 5.**
- **"Fix the mechanism, not the person"** follows the system approach described by Reason (2000); making it a fixed repair step in a use, misuse, disuse and abuse incident record is Trigpoint's (2026). The research supports looking upstream (abuse) and doubting fixes that rely on attention or instructions.

## How the references were checked

Each reference except Reason (2000) was taken from a node of the Human-Automation Interaction shelf at trigpoint.pub, where its bibliography was checked before publication. The verification status in `references/basis-index.md` is the status shown on that node's page. Reason (2000) was added in November 2026 after a prior-art check and was read in full in a reprint, not the publisher's page.

## Third-party material

The skill text, the record template and the practice incidents are written for this repository. The basis index reproduces each node's title and one-line intro from the public theory layer of the shelf, which is open for reuse with attribution and a link to the page; each row carries that link. No figures, tables or text from the papers are reproduced.
